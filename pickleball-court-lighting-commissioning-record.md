# Pickleball Court Lighting Commissioning Record Template

Use this field record after installation and aiming. It gives the owner, electrical contractor and lighting supplier one consistent place to confirm what was installed, which operating scene was tested and how the results were measured. It is a documentation template, not a substitute for local code, an adopted lighting standard or a professional electrical design.

## 3. Aiming and control verification

- Confirm every fixture is mounted at the approved coordinate and height.
- Record the final aiming reference for each fixture or pole group.
- Confirm shields and guards are installed in the approved orientation.
- Test each court or group control zone separately.
- Record the scene level used for measurements.
- Test schedules, overrides and the safe state after a booking ends.
- Note any fixture, circuit or control point that does not respond as documented.

## 7. Deviations and corrective work

For every deviation, record:

1. the affected fixture, court, grid point or control zone;
2. the approved value or arrangement;
3. the installed observation;
4. the corrective action;
5. the drawing or calculation revision, when a design change is required;
6. the remeasurement result and approval date.

Avoid undocumented field aiming. If corrective aiming changes the approved arrangement, update the aiming schedule and retain the final calculation or field record.

## 8. Handover package

The final package should include the approved drawings, fixture schedule, photometric calculation, point-by-point measurement record, aiming schedule, control narrative, product data, electrical documents assigned by the project team, warranty information and the signed deviation log.

For a companion technical checklist, see the [AHFL pickleball court lighting standards and procurement guide](https://aihuafeili.com/pickleball-court-lighting-standards/). Project-specific quantities, optics, mounting and aiming still require the actual venue geometry and applicable local requirements.
